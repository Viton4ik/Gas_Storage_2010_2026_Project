# Description

Reported gas inventories from January 2010 through August 2026, in billion
standard cubic metres. The largest reporter stays pinned at the top. Six
countries race in the main chart. A lower comparison row shows the smallest
strictly positive reported stock for each displayed month.

The lower country is selected from every eligible reporter, not from a fixed
pair. Zeros and missing data are excluded. Up to seven decimal places keep
small positive stock levels from being rounded to zero. Ties retain the
current country where possible. The lower value is the actual month-end
observation; the main race uses interpolated animation between monthly points.

Key source-month observations:
- January 2010: United States 65.238, Italy 9.353, France 6.503 bcm.
- January 2016: United States 83.197, Canada 22.220, Netherlands 16.250 bcm.
- December 2020: United States 94.606, Canada 24.351, Germany 18.426 bcm.
- August 2026: United States 92.982, Canada* 25.793, Italy 16.058 bcm.

These measure recoverable gas already in storage, including the types of gas
stocks covered by the reporting system. They are not proved reserves in
fields, maximum storage capacity, or a separately identified government
strategic reserve. Reported stock levels exclude cushion gas under the JODI
definition; national reporting practices may still affect comparability.

Coverage is incomplete and changes over time. Assessment codes 1 and 2 are
retained; unassessed code-3 observations are excluded. Russia's stock series
is therefore excluded, and China has no matching closing-stock series in the
extract. Germany enters in 2013, Netherlands in 2014 and Canada in 2016.
Ukraine's reporting series ends in January 2022. Entries/exits represent
reporting coverage. No missing history is backcast or replaced with zero.

An asterisk indicates a source caution qualification. Canada's August 2026
observation is code 2, "use with caution"; its cause remains unresolved.
Lower-row code-2 winners are also marked. This is a ranking of the screened
reports available for each month, rather than a complete world ranking.

Source: JODI-Gas, publication 28, downloaded 18 November 2026.
- Download: https://www.jodidata.org/jodi-publisher/gas/28/GAS_world_NewFormat.zip
- Database: https://www.jodidata.org/gas/database/data-downloads.aspx
- Definitions: https://www.jodidata.org/gas/support/user-guide/data-available-in-the-jodi-gas-world-database.aspx
- Assessment codes: https://www.jodidata.org/_resources/files/downloads/gas-data/jodi-gas-wdb-short--long-names-ver2025.pdf

Timing: 120 seconds with slower dense years and short settling holds. Every
source month is retained. Music: original track generated for this video.
